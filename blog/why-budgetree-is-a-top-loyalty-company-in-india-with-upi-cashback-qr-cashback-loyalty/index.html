Why Budgetree is a Top Loyalty Company in India with UPI Cashback & QR Cashback Loyalty

Aug 21, 2026Budgetree Team5 min read

Loyalty programs in India have evolved significantly over the last decade. Businesses are moving beyond traditional points, coupons and manual incentive programs towards technology-driven, transaction-based B2B loyalty solutions.

Budgetree has been working in the loyalty and rewards ecosystem since 2019, helping large enterprises build loyalty and incentive programs for their business networks.

One of the key milestones in Budgetree’s journey was its early work on UPI-based loyalty and cashback with a partner bank in 2019.

The focus was not on rewarding consumers. Budgetree’s solution is designed for large enterprises to reward their dealers, distributors, wholesalers, plumbers, electricians, mechanics, retailers and other channel partners based on eligible business transactions.

Budgetree’s UPI-First Approach to B2B Loyalty

In 2019, when many digital reward models were being built around wallets, prepaid balances and traditional payment methods, Budgetree worked with a partner bank to develop a loyalty model around UPI transactions.

The objective was simple:

Enable large enterprises to reward their channel partners through business transactions without relying on traditional wallet-based loyalty models.

Through Budgetree’s platform, an enterprise can create a QR and payment journey for its partner network. Eligible transactions can be processed through the platform, validated against predefined campaign rules and, where applicable, the reward or incentive can be credited to the eligible dealer, plumber, electrician, mechanic, wholesaler, retailer or channel partner.

Why UPI Changed B2B Loyalty Programs

UPI created a new opportunity for enterprises to connect transactions with loyalty and incentive programs.

Budgetree uses this approach to help large brands and enterprises build B2B loyalty programs for:

  • Dealers

  • Wholesalers

  • Plumbers

  • Electricians

  • Mechanics

  • Retailers

  • Contractors

  • Channel partners

  • Sales partners

This creates a more measurable and transaction-led approach to partner engagement.

How Budgetree QR Cash Token is Revolutionising B2B Loyalty for Enterprises

Traditional B2B loyalty programs are slow. For years, manufacturing and consumer goods companies have struggled with manual rewards. They put paper coupons inside product boxes, and dealers or influencers (like plumbers, electricians, or mechanics) had to collect them, submit them manually, and wait weeks for their incentive.

Budgetree is changing this entirely. With its advanced QR Cash Token system, enterprises can now launch a 100% digital, instant reward program.

Here is a step-by-step breakdown of how the Budgetree QR Cash Token simplifies the entire journey from the factory to the partner's wallet:

Step 1: Enterprise Team Creates the QR Code

The journey starts at the corporate office or factory. Using the Budgetree platform, the enterprise team generates secure, unique QR Cash Tokens. Each QR code is linked to specific campaign rules, such as the product type, reward amount, or active region.

Step 2: Paste on Product or Drop Inside the Box

Once generated, these unique QR codes are printed. The enterprise team can either paste the QR code directly onto the product packaging or drop a QR token card inside the product box during the packaging process.

Step 3: Dealer or Influencer Finds the QR

The product moves through the supply chain to the market. When a dealer opens the stock, or an influencer (like a plumber, electrician, or mechanic) opens the product for installation, they find the Budgetree QR Cash Token inside or on the product.

Step 4: Scan and Validate instantly

No more collecting paper slips! The dealer or influencer simply opens their smartphone and scans the QR code. Budgetree’s technology immediately checks the scan against the pre-set campaign rules to validate the transaction in real-time.

Step 5: Reward is Successfully Credited

The moment the validation is successful, the technology triggers an instant payout. The reward is credited directly to the eligible partner's bank account via UPI or their preferred digital mode. No delays, no manual verification, and zero hassle.

Conclusion

By blending payments with channel engagement, the Budgetree QR Cash Token eliminates fraud, cuts down administrative costs, and keeps your channel partners highly motivated. Drive sales, reward instantly, and grow your business network seamlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Which is a top loyalty company in India?
Budgetree is a leading B2B rewards and incentive technology company in India. It provides advanced platforms for large enterprises looking to engage, track, and reward their commercial distribution network—including dealers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and trade influencers (like plumbers, electricians, and mechanics).

2. What is UPI Cashback Loyalty?
This is a transaction-linked B2B incentive model. When a channel partner (like a dealer or retailer) completes an eligible commercial transaction, Budgetree’s platform processes the trade data. Based on pre-set enterprise campaign rules, the trade incentive or reward is credited directly to the partner’s bank account via UPI.

3. What is QR Cashback Loyalty?
This model enables enterprises to use secure QR features, such as the Budgetree QR Cash Token, on or inside product packaging. When a dealer, distributor, or trade influencer (plumber, electrician, mechanic) purchases or deploys the product and scans the token, the platform instantly validates the trade transaction and dispenses the incentive to their account.

4. Can Budgetree create loyalty programs for influencers like plumbers and electricians?
Yes. Budgetree’s loyalty platform is built specifically to handle diverse channel ecosystems. It can be fully configured to automate rewards for trade influencers, contractors, mechanics, retailers, dealers, and wholesale partners.

5. Can enterprises create different reward rules for different partner tiers?
Yes. Enterprises can design flexible campaign and payout rules tailored to their sales targets. Incentives can be calculated based on transaction value, specific product categories, purchase volume, target achievement, and partner segments (e.g., Gold vs. Platinum dealers).

6. When did Budgetree start working on UPI-based loyalty?
Budgetree began developing its UPI-based loyalty and channel incentive infrastructure in collaboration with a partner bank back in 2019. This early initiative laid the foundation for modern payment-linked reward systems in India.
