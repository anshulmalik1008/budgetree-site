Budgetree Unveils Gift Card API to Issue Digital Gift Cards from 350+ Brands

Enterprises, fintechs, and loyalty platforms can now issue, deliver, and track digital gift cards through Budgetree’s REST-based Gift Card API—built for corporate gifting, rewards, and high-volume campaigns.

10 Jul 2026India2 min read

Budgetree Technology Pvt. Ltd. today announced the availability of its Gift Card API, enabling developers and product teams to issue, deliver, and track digital gift cards from 350+ brands through a single secure integration.

The API is designed for loyalty platforms, HR tech, fintech apps, marketplaces, and corporate gifting programs that need enterprise-grade automation without managing multiple vendor integrations.

Key Capabilities

  • Catalog browse and brand discovery across fashion, food, entertainment, travel, electronics, and more
  • Instant issuance with delivery via email, SMS, or in-app redemption flows
  • Fixed or custom-value denominations with configurable limits and expiry rules
  • Webhooks for issuance, redemption, failures, and balance updates
  • Sandbox environment, RESTful JSON APIs, and reconciliation reports for ops and finance teams

Built for Enterprise Use Cases

Organizations can use the Gift Card API to automate employee milestone rewards, power B2B gifting portals, redeem loyalty points into brand gift cards, or offer gift cards as perks and cashback alternatives inside wallets and apps.

Budgetree handles fulfillment behind the scenes while partners retain white-label control over the customer experience.

Security & Reliability

The API supports OAuth 2.0, API keys, IP allowlisting, encryption in transit, and audit logs—aligned with the needs of regulated industries. Budgetree targets enterprise-grade uptime and sub-two-second average issuance times.

Availability

Teams can request sandbox access, technical documentation, and a solution walkthrough by contacting sales@budgetree.in or booking a demo on budgetree.in.
