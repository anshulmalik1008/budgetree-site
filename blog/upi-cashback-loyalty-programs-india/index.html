India’s First UPI Cashback on Product — Powered by Budgetree

What if your customers could receive instant cashback directly on UPI after purchasing your brand’s products?

Budgetree introduced the concept of UPI-based cashback on products in India in 2020, creating a new way for brands to connect product purchases, customer engagement and instant rewards.

Today, UPI cashback can become a powerful tool for brands looking to increase product sales, encourage repeat purchases and build stronger customer relationships.

What Is Product-Based UPI Cashback?

Product-based UPI cashback is a reward mechanism where a customer receives cashback after purchasing an eligible product or completing a qualifying transaction.

The journey can be simple:

Buy Product → Verify Purchase → Customer Becomes Eligible → Cashback Triggered → UPI Reward

Instead of waiting for points or manually redeeming a voucher, customers can receive a monetary reward through UPI.

Why Launch UPI Cashback on Your Products?

Traditional promotions often provide discounts at the time of purchase.

UPI cashback can create a different experience:

Purchase First → Reward After

This gives brands the flexibility to run targeted reward campaigns without permanently reducing the product's displayed price.

Increase Product Sales

Brands can use cashback as a purchase incentive for selected products, SKUs, locations or campaigns.

Encourage Repeat Purchases

Rewarding customers after qualifying purchases can help create a stronger reason to return to the brand.

Promote New Products

Launching a new SKU? UPI cashback can be used as an introductory incentive to encourage trial.

Reward Customer Loyalty

Cashback can become part of a larger loyalty strategy, where customers earn rewards for purchasing specific products or reaching campaign milestones.

Run Targeted Campaigns

Brands can design campaigns around:

  • Specific products

  • Selected SKUs

  • Purchase quantities

  • Retailers

  • Locations

  • Customer segments

  • Campaign periods

  • Sales targets

UPI Cashback + QR Code

One of the most effective ways to connect physical products with digital rewards is through QR-based engagement.

A customer can scan a campaign QR code and complete the required steps to become eligible for the reward.

The journey can be:

Product → QR Scan → Verification → Eligibility → UPI Cashback

This gives brands a direct digital engagement point with customers.

UPI Cashback + Loyalty

UPI cashback does not have to work as a standalone promotion.

It can become part of a complete loyalty ecosystem.

For example:

Purchase → Earn Points → Unlock Cashback → Receive UPI Reward → Continue Engagement

Budgetree can help businesses bring together loyalty, rewards, cashback, incentives and payouts within a broader customer engagement strategy.

Instant Value

Customers receive a reward they can use rather than waiting for a traditional redemption cycle.

Simple Customer Experience

UPI is already familiar to millions of Indian consumers.

Better Campaign Flexibility

Brands can define their own products, eligibility criteria and campaign mechanics.

Measurable Engagement

Digital campaigns can provide better visibility into participation, rewards and campaign performance.

Scalable Rewards

UPI cashback can be used for campaigns ranging from targeted promotions to large-scale customer programs.
